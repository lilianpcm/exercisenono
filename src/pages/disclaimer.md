General information, not medical advice. Check with your doctor before starting a new exercise program.

The guides, charts and printables on Exercise No No are **general fitness information for educational purposes**. They are not medical advice and do not replace advice from a doctor, physical therapist or other qualified professional.

- **Check with your doctor** before starting a new exercise routine, especially if you are pregnant, recovering from an injury or surgery, or have a health condition.
- **Listen to your body.** Stop right away if you feel pain, dizziness, chest discomfort or shortness of breath, and seek medical help if needed.
- **You exercise at your own risk.** Choose moves and weights that suit your fitness level, and use stable furniture and safe equipment.
- **We make no promises of results.** Results vary from person to person.

Our printables are **digital PDF files** that you download and print at home. No physical item is shipped.
